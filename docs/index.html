About Barcode Vault

This app stores all your scanned barcode data locally on your own device only, inside your browser's storage. No barcode values, labels, or images are ever sent to or stored on any external server or database.

Warning: your barcode data lives only in this browser on this device. If you clear your browser's site data, or storage in your phone's settings — or uninstall/reset this browser — your saved barcodes will be permanently lost. There is no backup or recovery.

We have made every reasonable effort to ensure there is no data leak or unintended exposure of your information.
